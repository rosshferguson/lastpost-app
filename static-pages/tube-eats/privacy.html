Privacy Policy

Tube Eats  ·  Last updated October 2026

Overview

Tube Eats is a simple utility app. We do not collect, store or share any personal data. No account is required to use the app.

Location data

When you use the Near You feature, Tube Eats requests access to your device's location. Your location is used only to find nearby venues in the moment — it is never stored, logged or transmitted to us.

You can revoke location access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Tube Eats.

Venue data

Venue information (names, addresses, ratings, opening hours and photos) is fetched from the Google Places API and cached temporarily on your device for up to 24 hours to reduce data usage. This data is stored locally and is not transmitted to us.

Google's privacy policy applies to data sent to the Places API: policies.google.com/privacy

Favourites

Venues you save to Favourites are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics and advertising

Tube Eats contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Children

Tube Eats does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app contains no user-generated content or social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
