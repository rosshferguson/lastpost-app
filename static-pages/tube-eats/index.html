📖 How to use Tube Eats

  • By station — search or browse for any Tube, Overground, DLR or Elizabeth line stop to find nearby food and drink.
  • Near You — tap Near You on the home screen to find venues around your current location. Choose a walking or driving radius to narrow results.
  • Search by food type — enter a cuisine (e.g. sushi, curry) to find which stations have the best options nearby.
  • Filters — narrow results by outlet type, cuisine, price range, and whether places are open right now.
  • Favourites — tap the heart on any venue to save it for later.

❓ Frequently asked questions

  • Why are no results showing? Make sure your internet connection is active. Location permission is required for the Near You feature — check Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Tube Eats.
  • Results seem outdated. Venue data is cached for 24 hours. Reinstalling the app clears the cache and fetches fresh results.
  • A venue is listed incorrectly. Venue details come from Google Places. You can suggest an edit directly in Google Maps.
  • Why is the app UK only? Tube Eats is designed specifically for the London Underground network.
