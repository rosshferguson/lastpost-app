Medicinal
The medical word game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Medicinal does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

Medicinal is a daily word game. It is designed to work entirely on your device with no accounts, no sign-in, and no internet connection required to play.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. Medicinal does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers of any kind.

What Is Stored on Your Device

Medicinal stores the following information locally on your device only, using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults):

• Your game progress for the current day's puzzle
• Your statistics (games played, win streak, guess distribution)
• Your current game state so you can resume mid-game

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server, not accessible to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

Medicinal does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. No third-party SDKs are included.

Internet Access

Medicinal does not make any network requests. No data is sent to or received from any server. The only exception is if you choose to tap "View on BNF" on the result screen, which opens the British National Formulary website in your browser — this is a standard external link and is governed by the BNF's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Medicinal does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how data is handled (for example, if a future version adds optional features), this policy will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at rossmrpharms@gmail.com.
