# App Store Description — Last Post

Paste this into App Store Connect → App Information → Description.

---

Last Post helps you ensure your loved ones are notified when you pass away — without putting that burden on them in their most difficult moment.

Add the people you want notified, leave personal messages for each of them, and appoint a trusted Designated Person to trigger notifications when the time comes. Last Post handles the rest.

**What Last Post does:**
• Notifies your chosen contacts by email or SMS
• Lets you record funeral wishes, important documents, and digital accounts
• Gives your Designated Person access to everything they need
• Sends regular wellness checks so your designated person knows you're OK
• Includes a web trigger link as a backup if the app isn't available
