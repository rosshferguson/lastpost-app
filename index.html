How it works

Simple, dignified, and private.

Set it up once and Last Post takes care of everything at the right time.

1

Add your contacts

Add the people you'd like to be notified — family, friends, colleagues. They'll be invited to confirm their details.

2

Choose a designated person

Appoint someone you trust to trigger the notification process when the time comes. They'll have a dedicated view in the app.

3

Add legacy planning information

Record your funeral wishes, important documents, digital assets, and life history — all stored securely and shared with your designated person when the time comes.

4

Last Post handles the rest

Your designated person confirms the news and every contact on your list receives a personal, compassionate notification.
