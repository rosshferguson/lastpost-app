## Description *(plain text — App Store doesn't render markdown)*

Nobody wants to think about this. But the alternative — leaving your loved ones to piece things together in the middle of grief — is worse.

Last Post lets you sort it now, quietly, so you never have to think about it again.

HOW IT WORKS

Add the people you want notified when you pass away — family, friends, colleagues, anyone who matters. Each person receives a personal invitation to join your list, so they know what to expect when the time comes.

Choose someone you trust completely as your Designated Person. When the time comes, they complete a secure, step-by-step process — and everyone on your list receives a personal email notification. No guesswork, no contacts missed.

A built-in waiting period and multi-step confirmation prevent any accidental sends.

LEGACY PLANNING

Go further than just a contact list. Record your funeral wishes, note where your important documents are kept, capture your life story, and list your digital accounts and assets — all in one private place.

Share photos and personal messages with the people who'll treasure them most. Grant your Designated Person access to your arrangements so they have everything they need, when they need it.

PEACE OF MIND, BUILT IN

Preview exactly what your contacts will receive before anything is sent. Set up an annual check-in reminder to keep your list current. Enable a wellness check so your Designated Person hears from you regularly.

Your data is stored securely on your device and protected by Face ID.

PREMIUM

Last Post is free to get started. Premium unlocks unlimited contacts, multiple Designated Persons, and the full legacy planning suite.

## Notes for capturing

- Use **Light Mode** for all shots (cleaner, better contrast for App Store thumbnails)

- Use realistic-sounding names, not "Test" or "AAA"
- If any screen shows your real email/phone, swap it for a prop before screenshotting
