Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Short version: Meds Monitor stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal or health information on our servers. We have no servers.

Introduction

Meds Monitor ("the app", "we", "our") is a medication tracking and reminder app developed by Ross Ferguson. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is stored, and your rights in relation to it.

By using Meds Monitor, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Meds Monitor does not collect personal information in the traditional sense. The app works entirely on your device. The information you enter — names of people, medicines, and dose records — is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and never leaves your device unless you choose to sync via iCloud (see below).

Information you enter into the app

  • Names of people whose medicines you are tracking
  • Medicine names, dosing schedules, and dose records
  • Notes and "given by" initials on dose records
  • Stock counts and prescriber information (Care Home Mode)

This information is stored on your device only. We cannot access it.

Location data

The Pharmacy Finder feature uses your device's location to find nearby pharmacies. Location data is used in real time to make this search and is not stored or transmitted to us. Location access is permission-gated — the app will ask for your permission before accessing it, and you can revoke this at any time in iOS Settings.

Subscription and payment data

If you subscribe to Meds Monitor Premium, payment is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment card information. Apple provides us with a non-identifiable receipt confirming your subscription status.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your Meds Monitor data may sync between your own devices via Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. This is a direct device-to-device sync using your personal iCloud account — the data goes through Apple's servers, not ours. Apple's privacy policy governs how iCloud data is handled: apple.com/legal/privacy.

You can disable iCloud sync for Meds Monitor at any time in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect or store your health or medication data on any server
  • We do not share your data with any third parties
  • We do not display advertising or use your data for marketing
  • We do not use analytics tools that track your behaviour in the app
  • We do not require you to create an account to use the free version of the app
  • We do not sell data to data brokers

NHS Open Data

The Pharmacy Finder feature retrieves pharmacy location data from the NHS Open Data API. This is a public dataset. No personal information is sent to the NHS API — only a location query to return nearby results.

Children's Privacy

Meds Monitor is designed to help adults track medicines for themselves and their dependants, including children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Names entered to identify a child in the app are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data in Meds Monitor exists on your device. You can delete individual dose records within the app, or delete the app entirely to remove all associated data. If iCloud sync is enabled, deleting the app from all your devices will remove the iCloud data as well. To remove only the iCloud copy, disable iCloud sync for Meds Monitor in iOS Settings before deleting the app.

Medical Disclaimer

Meds Monitor is a tracking and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always follow the guidance of your doctor, pharmacist, or prescriber when administering medicines. The dosing intervals shown in the app are based on manufacturer guidance and are provided for reference only.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
